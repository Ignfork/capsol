Before you buy

Questions

Read the docs
Do I have to stake or lock my coins?

No. You just hold them in your own wallet. Tickets are read from your balance across the window.

What happens if I sell before the window ends?

Your tickets for that window are void and your streak resets. You keep everything you sold for, and nothing is taken.

How is the winner picked?

Five minutes before opening, the ticket list freezes and its hash is published. Chainlink VRF then supplies a random seed, and the winning index is that seed modulo the total ticket count. Anyone can replay it in the Proof Replayer on the Winners page.

Why square-root tickets?

So one whale can't buy the whole draw. $50 is one unit and tickets are the square root of units, which makes small and mid-size holders count.

Where is the $CAPSOL contract address?

It's posted on our X at launch. It will also appear on the $CAPSOL page here once it's live.

Is this an investment?

No. Capsol is not an investment product. Memecoins can go to zero, and only one holder is paid per window.

proof_replayer.exe · new?

Check any draw yourself. Paste the frozen ticket list and the VRF seed from a proof. This tool hashes the list, combines it with the seed and finds the winning ticket. It runs entirely in your browser.

The prefilled values are a made-up example for testing the method. They are not a real draw.

StatusWaiting for input
Method
  1. List hash = SHA-256 of the frozen list, one line per wallet.
  2. Draw hash = SHA-256 of seed + ":" + list hash.
  3. Index = draw hash as a number, modulo total tickets.
  4. Walk the list adding tickets. The wallet whose range holds the index wins.

How Capsol works

01_windows.txt

Windows

Fees collect for one window and the draw happens at its end. Windows line up to UTC, so a 4-hour coin opens at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00.

T+0:00Window opens
T+1:0025% hold mark
T+3:55List freezes, hash posted
T+4:00VRF draw
T+4:01Payout + proof

Your balance is time-weighted (TWAB) across the window, so a last-second buy doesn't count fully.

02_tickets.txt

Tickets

  • $50 of time-weighted holdings is one unit.
  • Tickets = √units, rounded down.
  • You must hold for at least 25% of the window.
  • Streak bonus: +10% per full window held in a row, up to +50%.
  • No staking. Your coins never leave your wallet.

Example: buy 2 minutes before the freeze and you get 0 tickets. Hold $16,900 for four full windows and you get √338 × 1.4 = 25 tickets.

03_winners.txt

Winners

Each coin's capsule pays one winner per window. $CAPSOL's hourly revenue draw is the exception and pays five.

04_fees.txt

Fees

  • Platform: 1% fixed on every trade. 30% of that goes to $CAPSOL holders.
  • Pot: 0.5–2%, set by the creator.
  • Creator: 0–1.5%, set by the creator.
  • Pot plus creator is capped at 2%.
  • Launching a coin costs 0.03 SOL.
05_proof.txt

Proof

Every opening publishes four things together: the frozen list hash, the VRF seed, the winning index and the payout transaction signature. Paste them into the Proof Replayer to check a draw yourself.

06_disclosure.txt

Disclosure

For the minutes between a draw and its payout, the pot sits in a wallet Capsol controls. The payout signature in each proof shows when it left.

07_risk.txt

Risk

Capsol is not an investment product. Buying any coin can lose all of the money you put in. Only one holder is paid per window, so most holders win nothing in any given window. Capsol may not be available where you live.

08_terminal.txt

Terminal commands

The terminal on the home page runs real commands. Press / anywhere to jump to it.

  • help lists every command
  • next shows the next openings
  • odds 500 shows tickets for $500 held
  • fees, rules and capsol print the specs
  • go explore (or launch, openings, winners, docs) changes page
  • phosphor green / blue / solana changes the screen tint
  • crt off turns off scanlines
  • sound on turns on retro beeps
